## Q11 · Do the top 5 countries hold at least half of international tourists?

*Belief:* "International visitors come from a few countries."  
*Question:* Do the top 5 countries hold at least half of international tourists?  
*Data:* T8 · *Metric:* Share of international presences by country, tourists and excursionists; leading country per neighbourhood  
*Decision rule:* Same as Q3: a few countries dominate if the top 5 hold at least 50% of international presences.  

**Answer.** Among international overnight tourists the top 5 countries hold 52.0% (United States 12.7%, United Kingdom 11.9%, France 9.8%, Germany 9.3%, Italy 8.4%). In 2024 the leading country is France in 43 of 73 neighbourhoods, United States in 13.  
**Verdict: A few countries dominate.** Belief holds: **Yes**.  
*Limitation:* Small countries are grouped as NA in each neighbourhood.

## Q12 · Is Madrid at least half of national visitors?

*Belief:* "National visitors are mostly from Madrid."  
*Question:* Is Madrid at least half of national visitors?  
*Data:* T7 · *Metric:* Share of national presences by province of residence  
*Decision rule:* National visitors are MOSTLY FROM MADRID if Madrid is at least 50% of national presences (T7). Madrid is the LARGEST single origin if it ranks first.  

**Answer.** Top provinces of national visitors (presences): Girona 16.8%, Madrid 16.8%, Tarragona 14.9%, Lleida 6.8%, Balears, Illes 5.8%. Madrid is 16.8%; the other three Catalan provinces together 38.5%.  
**Verdict: Not mostly from Madrid (16.8%); Girona and Madrid are level as the largest.** Belief holds: **No**.  
*Limitation:* 'National' excludes the province of Barcelona (those are regional).

## Q18 · Are at least 16 of the 20 main country markets summer markets (June-August >= 1.30x the other months)?

*Belief:* "Foreign tourists all come in summer."  
*Question:* Are at least 16 of the 20 main country markets summer markets (June-August >= 1.30x the other months)?  
*Data:* T8 · *Metric:* June-August / other nine months per country (full years 2023-2024); seasonal index per month; peak and lowest month  
*Decision rule:* A country market is a SUMMER MARKET if its June-August monthly mean is at least 1.30 times the mean of the other nine months (the Q2 threshold, full years 2023 and 2024). The belief holds if at least 16 of the 20 main markets (80%) are summer markets; PARTLY if 10 to 15; NO if fewer than 10.  

**Answer.** 6 of the 20 main markets are summer markets (June-August >= 1.30x the other months): Germany 1.45x, United Kingdom 1.44x, France 1.40x, United States 1.39x, Mexico 1.39x, Netherlands 1.39x. The least seasonal markets peak outside summer: Japan 0.65x (peak Oct), Greece 0.79x (peak Mar), Korea S, Republic of 0.79x (peak Dec), Portugal 1.05x (peak Apr), China 1.06x (peak Oct). The 20 markets hold 82.7% of international presences.  
**Verdict: Only 6 of 20 markets are summer markets.** Belief holds: **No**.  
*Limitation:* Presences, not people; small countries grouped as NA are left out.

## Q19 · Do the 20 main markets share at least 7 of their top-10 neighbourhoods, and do they have niches of their own?

*Belief:* "Every nationality goes to the same neighbourhoods."  
*Question:* Do the 20 main markets share at least 7 of their top-10 neighbourhoods, and do they have niches of their own?  
*Data:* T8 · *Metric:* Overlap of each market's top 10 with the international top 10; location quotient (LQ) per neighbourhood with a base of 1,000 presences per month  
*Decision rule:* A country market FOLLOWS the international pattern if at least 7 of its top-10 neighbourhoods are in the top 10 of all international visitors (the Q10 threshold). It has its OWN NICHE if a neighbourhood with at least 1,000 presences per month of that market has a location quotient >= 1.25. The belief holds if at least 16 of the 20 main markets follow the pattern and fewer than half have a niche; PARTLY if they follow it but at least half have a niche; NO if fewer than 16 follow it.  

**Answer.** 20 of the 20 main markets share at least 7 of their top-10 neighbourhoods with all international visitors (la Dreta de l'Eixample is first for 19 of them). 17 of 20 also have their own niche (LQ >= 1.25 with >= 1,000 presences per month): France in Torre Baró (2.99), Korea S, Republic of in el Carmel (2.80), China in Vallvidrera, el Tibidabo i les Planes (2.71), Netherlands in Sarrià (2.11), Japan in el Baix Guinardó (1.90), Argentina Republic in el Camp de l'Arpa del Clot (1.86).  
**Verdict: Same main neighbourhoods for every market, plus a niche of their own for most.** Belief holds: **Partly**.  
*Limitation:* Neighbourhood x country values exist by year only (no visitor type); LQ uses presences.
