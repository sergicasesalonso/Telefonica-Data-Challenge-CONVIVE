## 1. Data

**Source.** Smart Steps (Telefónica Tech), supplied by the professor under a signed NDA on 29 Oct 2026. Twelve datasets, Barcelona,
January 2023 to August 2025 (32 months): 384 monthly files, 15.3 GB, 168.8 million rows. The raw files stay on one team laptop
and are never committed, uploaded or shared.

| Dataset | Unit | Level | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Unique visitors | City × month × origin × type | Q2, Q3, Q5, Q8 |
| T2 | Unique visitors | City × day × origin | Q4, Q9 |
| T3 | Unique tourists | Municipality × week × nights | Q7 |
| T4 | Unique visitors | Neighbourhood × month × origin × type | Q1, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q10, Q16, I8 |
| T5 | Unique visitors | Neighbourhood × day × origin | Q4, explorer |

**Visitor origin.** *Internacional* (residence outside Spain), *Nacional* (rest of Spain outside the province of Barcelona) and
*Regional* (province of Barcelona outside the city). *Residente* appears only in the mobility products. **Visitor type.** *Turista*
(spends at least one night) and *Excursionista* (no night). Regional visitors are always excursionists.

**Geography.** 73 neighbourhoods (barris) in 10 districts. A neighbourhood code is `08019` + district + position inside the
district (9 digits). The correspondence with the official names is in `deliverables/iteration2/2.1.1_geographic_levels.csv` and is
checked every month (73 of 73 match).

## 2. Three measures that are never mixed

1. **Unique visitors** count people in a place and a period. They are not additive: a person seen in two neighbourhoods, or in two
   months, is counted in each. A longer period is shown as a monthly (or daily) mean.
2. **Presences** are sums of unique visitors across neighbourhoods (person × neighbourhood). They are used for shares across
   neighbourhoods and are always labelled as presences, never as people. Validation shows that summing neighbourhoods gives 1.95 to
   5.89 times the city's people.
3. **Trips** (M1) count journeys between neighbourhoods. One person can make several trips; trips and visitors are never combined in
   one indicator.

## 3. Privacy and missing values

- Any count below 15 is published by Telefónica as suppressed. Our outputs write it as `<15` and never show a value from 1 to 14.
- In M1 a published 0 means fewer than 15 trips (42.5% of rows). Trip totals are lower estimates; trip shares are given with bounds
  that treat each 0 as 0 to 14 trips.
- `NA` is a segment grouped by the privacy threshold (e.g. small countries). It is kept as its own category, never read as zero.
- Three days are missing in every daily file (2024-02-11, 2025-04-13, 2025-05-20). Nothing is imputed; the months are flagged.

## 5. From a belief to a verdict

Each research question tests a common belief about visitors in Barcelona. For every question we fix, **before computing the answer**:
the dataset, the metric and a numeric decision rule (stored in `research.THRESHOLDS`). The code computes the metric, applies the rule
and writes the verdict and whether the belief holds (Yes, Partly, No). Changing a threshold changes a verdict but never the numbers,
and both are published. The thresholds are the team's and are pending validation by the Business Manager (change C12).

Main metrics: **N50** (smallest number of neighbourhoods holding half of the presences), top-10 share and Gini (concentration);
ratio of June-August to the other months (seasonality); weekend vs weekday change with a 95% bootstrap interval; January-August
growth between equivalent periods; visitor share of incoming trips; **location quotient** (share of an origin in a neighbourhood
divided by its share in the city); Spearman rank correlation; deviation from the median of the same weekday and month (unusual days).

## 6. Known limitations

- T9 has no international visitors (I9): the profile covers national and regional visitors only.
- M3 repeats a key in 43.7% of rows (I7) and is not used until the Business Manager explains it.
- Six neighbourhoods have very few data (I8); Verdun shows a -83% step in August 2023 that suggests a change in measurement.
- 2025 covers January-August only; two growth intervals cannot prove a long-term trend.
- The data locate and date patterns; they cannot name causes.
