# 3.1.2 Data quality assessment

CONVIVE · Team 102_A · Iteration 2. Generated by `quality.run_quality()` on the full data (32 months, all 12 datasets). No data rows are shown.

Checks S1 to S4, C1 to C4 and X1 to X3 come from `data_review_plan.md`. The R1 trigger is more than 10% of records with nulls or inconsistencies
in a key file, or neighbourhood codes that do not match the map.

## Problems that matter for the dashboard

1. **Neighbourhood codes (R1, fixed).** The Iteration 1 rule "8019 + district + BARRI" matches only 6 of 73 codes, because BARRI is numbered across the city while Smart Steps numbers neighbourhoods inside each district. Real codes also have 9 digits (`080190101`). After the correspondence in 2.1.1, all 73 match. Without this fix the map would show wrong neighbourhoods.
2. **Censored trips in Q6 (R1, reported).** 42.5% of M1 rows are published zeros, meaning fewer than 15 trips: Internacional 55.2%; Nacional 64.6%; Regional 42.7%; Residente 17.3%. Visitor flows are hidden more often than residents' flows, so the published visitor share may be understated. 3.1.6 reports a bound.
3. **Missing days.** 2024-02-11, 2025-04-13, 2025-05-20 are missing in all daily files. Months flagged as incomplete: 202402 (1 day), 202504 (1 day), 202505 (1 day). There is no imputation.
4. **M3 repeated keys (R1, open).** 43.7% of M3 rows repeat a day × origin × route key, usually with different values. M3 is not used in Q1 to Q6, so the dashboard is not affected. To ask the mentor before Iteration 3 (Q13).
5. **Two neighbourhoods with unusual tourism counts (open).** Verdun (986 unique visitors per month) and la Teixonera (2,761) are far below the median neighbourhood (134,455). All 29 X1 violations, out of 11,622 cells, fall in them ({'Verdun': 26, 'la Teixonera': 3}), with monthly values of at most 116. The data cannot show the reason. Their values at the bottom of the rankings and in Q6 should be read with care. To ask the mentor.
6. **T9 has no international visitors**, although the description lists them. It affects Q14 in Iteration 3, not the dashboard.
7. **Trips and unique visitors stay separate.** The Q6 map uses M1 trips; all other views use tourism files.
