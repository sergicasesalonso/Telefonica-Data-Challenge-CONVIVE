# 4.2.0 Preliminary dashboard: notes

CONVIVE · Team 102_A · Iteration 2 · Owner: Pau Borrell Bullich

The dashboard is `app/dashboard.py` (Streamlit, local). Its logic is in `app/dashboard_data.py`, and `app/check_dashboard.py` tests it. It reads only `deliverables/iteration2/3.1.6_stats/*.csv` and the neighbourhood GeoJSON.

```bash
streamlit run app/dashboard.py
```

## Check SC-13: at least 5 questions answered

The dashboard answers **6 of the 15 questions (Q1 to Q6)**, so SC-13 is met.

| View | Question | Source table | Measure | How it respects the rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KPI tiles and stacked bars | Q3 | `city_monthly_by_origin_type.csv` (T1) | Unique visitors | With several months selected, the first tile is the monthly average and says so; months are never summed |
| Monthly evolution by origin | Q2 | `city_monthly_by_origin_type.csv` (T1) | Unique visitors | Lines break where months are not selected |
| Year comparison | Q5 | `city_monthly_by_origin_type.csv` (T1) | Unique visitors, mean per month | Uses only months present in every selected year |
| Change per neighbourhood | Q5 | `barrio_monthly_by_origin_type.csv` (T4) | Unique visitors, mean per month | First against last selected year; bases below 1,000 are left out |
| Weekday pattern | Q4 | `city_daily_by_origin.csv` (T2) | Unique visitors, mean per day | Never summed into weeks |
| Neighbourhood map and top-10 table | Q1 | `barrio_monthly_by_origin_type.csv` (T4) | Unique visitors, mean per month | The share is "of the ranking", not of the city |
| Visitor share of incoming trips (map and table) | Q6 | `barrio_incoming_trips_by_origen.csv` (M1) | Trips | Title says "Trips, not people"; denominator always has all four origins |

## Filters

| Filter | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Year | Every view. The year comparison needs at least two years and says so |
| Month range | Every view |
| Visitor origin | Every view. `Residente` affects only Q6, because tourism files have no residents |
| Visitor type | Every view except Q6, because M1 has no visitor type. The limitation under the Q6 title says so |

The weekday view is computed from the daily table and the year comparison from the monthly table, not from the pre-computed `city_weekday_by_origin.csv` and `barrio_yoy_jan_aug.csv`, because those cannot respond to all the filters. The pre-computed tables remain as fixed reference values.

## 4.2.1 Interactive maps

The two map views (Q1 and Q6) are the first version of 4.2.1, plus the Q5 change map. Standalone versions with fixed values: `4.2.1_map_visitors.html` and `4.2.1_map_visitor_share_trips.html`. They contain Plotly embedded, make no external requests and work without internet (tested with the network blocked).
