# 3.1.6 Preliminary descriptive statistics: headline numbers

CONVIVE · Team 102_A · Iteration 2. Generated by `descriptives.py` from `3.1.6_stats/`. Every number is computed by the code. The results describe patterns, not causes.

## Q1. How concentrated are visitors across the 73 neighbourhoods?

In the median month, the 10 highest neighbourhoods hold 50.2% of the neighbourhood ranking (range 46.2% to 55.2% over 32 months). 10 of 73 is 13.7% of the neighbourhoods. The median Gini across neighbourhoods is 0.590. The highest monthly averages are la Dreta de l'Eixample (1,843,274), el Barri Gòtic (1,267,529), la Sagrada Família (889,225); the lowest is Verdun (986, an unusually low value flagged in 3.1.2). Median top-10 share by origin: Internacional 62.1%, Nacional 47.6%, Regional 38.0%.

*Limitation:* Neighbourhood values are not additive: a person visiting two neighbourhoods counts in both, so this is a share of the ranking, not of the city total.

## Q2. How much do visitors change from month to month, by origin?

Peak-to-trough ratio of monthly unique visitors: 1.44 in 2023 (peak July 2023, low February 2023) and 1.40 in 2024 (peak July 2024, low January 2024). June to August average is 1.13 and 1.14 times the other months of 2023 and 2024. Ratio by origin in 2024: Internacional 1.87; Nacional 1.37; Regional 1.25.

*Limitation:* 2025 covers January to August only, so it has no full-year ratio. Monthly values of months with a missing day are flagged.

## Q3. What share of monthly visitors is international, national or regional, tourist or excursionist?

Share of the average month's unique visitors: Internacional 55.8% (range 43.1% to 66.2%), Regional 28.1%, Nacional 16.1%. Tourists are 34.7% and excursionists 65.3%. International tourists alone are 28.6%.

*Limitation:* Regional visitors are always excursionists by definition, which raises the excursionist share.

## Q4. Are daily visitors higher at weekends than on weekdays, and for which origins?

Mean daily unique visitors are 497,841 on weekend days and 539,023 on weekdays (-7.6%). Weekend difference by origin: Internacional +0.3%; Nacional -14.4%; Regional -13.0%. Highest day by origin: Internacional Friday; Nacional Thursday; Regional Friday.

*Limitation:* Means per day over 971 days; daily values cannot be summed into weekly totals, and 3 days are missing.

## Q5. Did visitors grow between equivalent periods (January to August)?

Mean monthly unique visitors, January to August: 4,276,902 in 2023, 4,494,996 in 2024 and 4,598,167 in 2025 (+5.1% and +2.3%). From 2023 to 2025 by origin: Internacional +16.1%; Nacional -7.3%; Regional +0.3%. Of 73 neighbourhoods, 53 show a higher value and 20 a lower one in 2025 than in 2023.

*Limitation:* Only the 8 equivalent months are compared, and February 2024, April 2025 and May 2025 each miss one day. Change over two intervals cannot show a long-term trend.

## Q6. What share of incoming trips is made by visitors, and by residents?

Visitors make 44.7% of published trips between neighbourhoods (bound 44.5% to 47.1%, treating each published 0 as 0 to 14 trips). Visitors make more than half of incoming trips in 11 of 73 neighbourhoods (bound 10 to 21). Highest: el Barri Gòtic (70.5%), Sant Pere, Santa Caterina i la Ribera (64.6%), la Barceloneta (60.5%). Lowest: Verdun (0.9%), la Teixonera (2.5%) (both flagged in 3.1.2).

*Limitation:* Trips, not people. Trips inside the same neighbourhood are excluded, and flows below 15 appear as 0, which hides more visitor flows than resident flows. The bound covers published zeros only; routes with no row in the file are not covered.
