# CONVIVE · Research Questions and Topics List

**WBS 3.2.0 · Iteration 2 increment** · Team 102_A (Convive Lab) · Version v0.1, 5 Nov 2026 · Owner: Sergi Cases Alonso

## 1. Purpose

After the Iteration 1 demo, the professor asked us to write a list of questions and topics of interest. They should include common beliefs ("myths") about tourists in Barcelona that the Smart Steps data can confirm, qualify or refute. This list is the input for:
- the descriptive statistics (WBS 3.1.6);
- the dashboard layout (4.1.1);
- the preliminary dashboard (4.2.0).

Success criterion SC-13 requires the preliminary dashboard to answer **at least 5** of these questions at the Iteration 2 demo on 9 Nov 2026.

Each question states the belief we test, the dataset, the metric, the chart and the main limitation. The questions are hypotheses to check. No answer is assumed before the data are analysed.

**Main research question (Project Charter).** How does the presence of regional, national and international visitors in Barcelona's neighbourhoods change by weekday, month and year (January 2023 to August 2025), compared with residents?

## 2. Rules that apply to every question

- **Trips are not visitors.** Mobility files (M1 to M3) count trips, and one person can make several. Tourism files (T1 to T9) count unique people. The two measures are never mixed in one indicator.
- **Unique visitors are not additive.** Adding days does not give the month, and adding neighbourhoods does not give the city. City totals come from T1 and T2, not from summing T4 and T5.
- **The privacy threshold applies.** In mobility, trips below 15 are published as 0. In tourism, segments below 15 people are grouped as NA. A 0 or an NA never means "nobody".
- **Residents appear only in mobility** (`origen = Residente`). Tourism files contain regional, national and international visitors only. Regional visitors are always excursionists.
- **Overnight stays (T3) exist at municipality level only**, for national and international visitors.
- **Patterns, not causes.** The answers describe differences and changes. They do not say that tourists cause anything.
