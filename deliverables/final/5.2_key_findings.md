## Charter question (Q0)

*Question:* Did the visitor share of incoming trips rise by at least 2 points in at least half of the 73 neighbourhoods between 2023 and 2025?  
*Decision rule:* Visitors GAIN GROUND in a neighbourhood if their share of incoming trips (M1, January-August) rises by at least 2 percentage points between 2023 and 2025, LOSE GROUND if it falls by at least 2 points, and are STABLE in between. The belief holds if they gain ground in at least 37 of 73 neighbourhoods (half); PARTLY if 19 to 36; NO if fewer.

**Answer.** Visitors made 43.8% of the trips between neighbourhoods in January-August 2023, 45.1% in 2024 and 45.9% in 2025. Trips per day changed by +11.5% for visitors and +2.4% for residents between 2023 and 2025 (international +26.2%, national -11.6%, regional +4.1%). Visitors gain ground in 39 of 73 neighbourhoods, are stable in 25 and lose ground in 9. In 2025 visitors dominate (above 60%) in 3 neighbourhoods, visitors and residents share 23 (40-60%) and residents dominate 47.

**Verdict: Visitors gain ground in 39 of 73 neighbourhoods and lose it in 9.** Belief "Visitors are gaining ground on residents across the neighbourhoods of Barcelona." holds: **Yes**, with the count near its threshold (37): 20 of the gains exceed the uncertainty of the privacy threshold, and 34 of 58 remain without the neighbourhoods that have a level shift in M1.

*Limitation:* Trips, not people; trips inside a neighbourhood are excluded; a published 0 means fewer than 15 trips, so each share has bounds; two intervals cannot prove a long-term trend; no cause can be inferred.
