### Can Peguera and el Turó de la Peira: evidence of a change of zones in April 2024

These two neighbourhoods are next to each other and shift in the same month in opposite directions, for visitors and for residents alike. Together they change much less (means of the six months before and after). Real visitors and residents would hardly swap neighbourhoods overnight; a change in how the network areas are assigned would do exactly this. It is the most concrete evidence we can give Telefónica for I8.
